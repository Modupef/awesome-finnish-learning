## Language Learning Apps

- [Anki](https://apps.ankiweb.net) - Spaced-repetition flashcards
- [Clozemaster](https://www.clozemaster.com) - Fill-in-the-blank sentences, great for learning case endings.
- [Drops](https://languagedrops.com) - Visual vocabulary sessions.
- [Duolingo](https://www.duolingo.com) - Good for consistency and basics.
- [Memrise](https://www.memrise.com) - Short clips of native speakers plus flashcards.
- [DuoCards](https://duocards.com/en/) - Spaced-repitition flashcards
- [Suomipassi](https://suomipassi.utu.fi/)
- [WordDive](https://www.worddive.com/app/landing)

## Dictionaries & Translators

- [Sanakirja.org](https://www.sanakirja.org) - Multilingual dictionary including Finnish ↔ English.
- [DeepL](https://www.deepl.com/translator) - Often more natural Finnish than other translators.
- [Reverso](https://www.reverso.net) - Translation with context examples.
- [Redfoxsanakirja](https://redfoxsanakirja.fi/) - Translation with context examples.
- [Verbix](https://www.verbix.com/finnish/)- Conjugation helper
- [Globse](https://glosbe.com/)- Online dictionary
- [Kieli.net](https://kieli.net/)

## Easy Finnish & Children's Books

- [Selkokeskus](https://selkokeskus.fi) - National Easy Finnish centre; book lists and info on *selkokirjat*.
- [Kirjasampo](https://www.kirjasampo.fi) - Finnish fiction database; search for *selkokirja* or children's books.
- [Kirjastot.fi](https://www.kirjastot.fi) - Find your local library for e-books and audiobooks.
